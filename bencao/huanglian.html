The ClassicThe original text

「味苦寒。主热气，目痛眦伤泣出，明目，肠澼腹痛下利，妇人阴中肿痛。久服令人不忘。」

Bitter in flavor, cold. It treats heat qi, eye pain with injured canthi and tearing, brightens the eyes, intestinal afflux with abdominal pain and diarrhea, and in women swelling and pain of the genitals. Taken over a long time, it makes one not forget.

RootBro's note — 'Stops vomiting' and 'treats dysentery' are huanglian's two clinical pillars. In Banxia Xiexin Tang, huanglian descends with its bitterness while dried ginger opens with its pungency — the 'pungent opening, bitter descending' paradigm for regulating the gut that shaped countless later formulas.
PhD InsightA systems-biology reading
Its key constituent berberine is one of the most thoroughly studied natural antibacterial compounds, with significant blood-sugar-lowering and gut-microbiome-regulating effects as well.
Berberine · 小檗碱
Inhibits bacterial DNA gyrase → broad-spectrum antibacterial action (gut pathogens). Activates the AMPK pathway → insulin sensitivity ↑ → blood sugar ↓. Regulates the gut microbiome → less endotoxin production → anti-inflammatory.
A modern reading of 'taken long, it makes one not forget': berberine inhibits acetylcholinesterase, raising acetylcholine levels in the synaptic cleft — a mechanism similar to donepezil, the Alzheimer's drug. The ancients' long-term observation, confirmed by modern pharmacology.
