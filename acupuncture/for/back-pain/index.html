Acupuncture for Back Pain and Sciatica: What It Can and Can't Do

Low back pain is the number-one reason Americans try acupuncture, and it is the condition with the strongest evidence behind it. The American College of Physicians recommends acupuncture as a first-line, non-drug treatment for chronic low back pain, and Medicare covers it for exactly that.

Does acupuncture help back pain?

For chronic low back pain, large reviews find that acupuncture reduces pain and improves function more than no treatment, and modestly more than sham needling. The effect is real but not magic: think of it as one tool next to movement, physical therapy and time, and one that has far fewer side effects than long-term pain medication.

What about sciatica?

Sciatica — pain running from the buttock down the leg, usually from an irritated nerve root — has less research than plain back pain, but several trials report less leg pain and better function with acupuncture. Practitioners often add electro-acupuncture (a mild current between needles) for nerve pain.

Which points are used?

Common choices are points along the lower back beside the spine, at the back of the knee (BL40, which classical texts call the command point of the back), and along the outer leg for sciatica. Our Pain Point Finder shows these by body area.

How many sessions?

A typical plan is one or two sessions a week for four to six weeks. Most people who will respond notice some change within the first four or five sessions. Medicare's own rule (up to 12 sessions in 90 days, 8 more if improving) is a reasonable benchmark.

Cost and insurance

Back pain is the condition insurers are most likely to cover. See is acupuncture covered by insurance and how much acupuncture costs.

See a doctor first if: back pain follows a fall or accident, comes with fever, unexplained weight loss, numbness in the groin, loss of bladder or bowel control, or leg weakness that is getting worse. These need medical care before anything else.

Questions, answered straight

Is acupuncture good for lower back pain?

Yes — it is the best-studied use of acupuncture. US physician guidelines list it as a first-line non-drug option for chronic low back pain, and Medicare covers it.

Can acupuncture make sciatica worse?

It is uncommon. Some people feel sore for a day after treatment. Pain that is sharply worse or new weakness should be checked by a doctor.

How quickly does acupuncture work for back pain?

Some people feel relief after the first session, but most need four to six sessions to judge whether it is working.
