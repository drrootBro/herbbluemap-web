Does Acupuncture Work? Honest Answers to the Questions Everyone Asks

These are the questions Americans type into Google before their first appointment, answered straight — including the parts where the evidence is thin.

Does acupuncture actually work?

For some things, yes. The strongest evidence is for chronic pain: low back pain, neck pain, knee osteoarthritis, tension headaches and migraine prevention. For nausea, anxiety, insomnia and many other uses, studies are encouraging but less consistent. Part of the benefit in trials comes from the ritual of care itself — but real acupuncture usually beats sham needling for pain, and both beat no treatment.

How does it work?

Needling stimulates nerves in skin and muscle, which changes how the spinal cord and brain process pain and releases the body's own pain-relieving chemicals. Chinese medicine describes the same effect as restoring the flow of qi through the channels. See acupuncture, explained.

What to expect at your first visit

A first appointment usually runs 60 to 90 minutes. Expect questions about your main complaint, sleep, digestion and medications; many practitioners also look at your tongue and take your pulse at both wrists (here is what they look for). Needles are hair-thin, sterile and single-use. Most people feel a brief pinch, then heaviness or warmth. Follow-ups are shorter, and for chronic problems a typical plan is weekly sessions for four to eight weeks before deciding whether it is working.

Questions, answered straight

Does acupuncture hurt?

Usually very little. The needles are hair-thin; most people feel a quick pinch, then a dull ache, heaviness or warmth.

Does acupuncture bleed or bruise?

A drop of blood or a small bruise sometimes happens when a needle is removed. It is harmless and fades in days.

Are acupuncture needles reused?

No. Licensed US acupuncturists use sterile, single-use disposable needles, which the FDA regulates as medical devices.

Do you have to undress for acupuncture?

Usually only enough to reach the points — rolled-up sleeves and pant legs are common. Clinics provide gowns or sheets when needed.

Can acupuncture make you tired?

Yes, many people feel relaxed or sleepy afterward. Plan a calm rest of the day after your first session.

Does acupuncture detox your body?

There is no good evidence that acupuncture removes toxins. Its studied effects are on pain, nerves and the stress response.

Is acupuncture the same as dry needling?

No. Dry needling targets muscle trigger points and is done by physical therapists and others; acupuncture uses a full system of points and is done by licensed acupuncturists.
