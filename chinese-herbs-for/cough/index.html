Chinese Herbs for Cough: What Chinese Medicine Uses and Why

A cough is a symptom, and Chinese medicine treats coughs very differently depending on the phlegm: none, thin and watery, or thick and yellow. Here is the classical map, with the formulas practitioners reach for.

Thin, watery phlegm, worse with cold

Cold-fluid cough. Classic choices are Xiao Qing Long Tang when chills are still present, and Ling Gan Wu Wei Jiang Xin Xia Ren Tang for the lingering cold-type cough. Key herbs: ban xia, xi xin, wu wei zi.

Thick, yellow phlegm, heat signs

Heat in the lung: Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang for cough and wheeze with heat; Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang for thick, foul phlegm.

Dry, tickly cough that lingers after a cold

The cough that hangs on for weeks with a dry throat. The classical formula is Mai Men Dong Tang, which moistens rather than dries.

Sore throat with cough

The two-herb Jie Geng Tang (platycodon and licorice) is the ancestor of many throat formulas.

Kitchen helpers

Pear steamed with a little rock sugar is the classic home remedy for a dry cough; fresh ginger tea for a chilly one. Honey soothes cough in adults and children over one year old (never give honey to babies under one).

Before you take Chinese herbs: herbs are medicine. They can interact with prescription drugs (blood thinners, blood-pressure, diabetes and psychiatric medications especially), and quality varies by supplier. Avoid them in pregnancy unless a qualified practitioner prescribes them, buy from suppliers that test for heavy metals, and tell your doctor what you take.
See a doctor for a cough lasting more than three weeks, coughing blood, shortness of breath, chest pain, wheezing that is new, night sweats or weight loss.

Questions, answered straight

What Chinese herb is good for a dry cough?

Practitioners use moistening herbs; the classic formula for a lingering dry cough is Mai Men Dong Tang (ophiopogon decoction). Steamed pear is the home version.

What Chinese medicine is good for cough with phlegm?

It depends on the phlegm: warming formulas such as Xiao Qing Long Tang for thin, watery phlegm; cooling formulas such as Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang for thick, yellow phlegm.

How long should a cough last before seeing a doctor?

If it lasts more than three weeks, or comes with blood, breathlessness or chest pain, see a doctor.
