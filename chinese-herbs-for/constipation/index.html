Chinese Herbs for Constipation: What Chinese Medicine Uses and Why

Chinese medicine splits constipation into two very different problems: a hot, full blockage that needs clearing, and a dry, weak bowel that needs moistening. Using a strong purgative for the second kind is a classic mistake.

Dry, weak constipation (older adults, after surgery, chronic)

The gentle classic is Ma Zi Ren Wan (hemp seed pill), built around oily seeds that moisten the bowel, with a small dose of rhubarb. It is one of the most widely sold Chinese patent formulas in the US.

Hot, full constipation (dry stool, bloating, bad breath)

The "Order the Qi" family, from mild to strong: Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang, Xiao Cheng Qi Tang and Da Cheng Qi Tang. Their engine is da huang (rhubarb root) with mang xiao, zhi shi and hou po. These are strong purgatives for short-term use under a practitioner — not for daily use, not in pregnancy.

When someone is both blocked and exhausted

For the patient who needs a purge but is too weak to bear one, later doctors added ginseng and angelica: Huang Long Tang.

Before any herb

Water, fiber, movement and a regular morning time on the toilet fix most constipation. Our Pain Point Finder also lists acupressure points traditionally used for sluggish bowels (such as ST25 beside the navel).

Before you take Chinese herbs: herbs are medicine. They can interact with prescription drugs (blood thinners, blood-pressure, diabetes and psychiatric medications especially), and quality varies by supplier. Avoid them in pregnancy unless a qualified practitioner prescribes them, buy from suppliers that test for heavy metals, and tell your doctor what you take.
See a doctor for blood in the stool, black stools, a sudden change in bowel habits after age 45, weight loss, severe abdominal pain, vomiting, or no bowel movement with a swollen belly — that can be an obstruction and is an emergency.

Questions, answered straight

What is the best Chinese herb for constipation?

For chronic, dry constipation the classic is Ma Zi Ren Wan (hemp seed pill). Rhubarb-based formulas are much stronger and meant for short-term use.

Is rhubarb root safe for constipation?

Short-term, under guidance. Long-term use of anthraquinone laxatives like rhubarb can make the bowel dependent; avoid in pregnancy.

Can Chinese medicine cure chronic constipation?

It can help, especially combined with diet and habits, but persistent constipation should be checked by a doctor first.
