Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang (苓桂术甘汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang — 苓桂术甘汤, the Poria, Cinnamon Twig, Atractylodes, and Licorice Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: heart and Spleen yang deficiency, retained phlegm-fluid surging upward.

Ingredients of Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang

  • Fu Ling (poria fungus) — 12g
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 9g · bark removed
  • Bai Zhu (white atractylodes rhizome) — 6g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Heart and Spleen yang deficiency, retained phlegm-fluid surging upward.

Typical pulse: submerged, tight pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Chronic congestive heart failure, Ménière's disease, orthostatic hypotension.

The original line

With fullness and counterflow fullness below the heart, qi surging upward into the chest, and dizziness upon standing: Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang governs it.

心下逆满，气上冲胸，起则头眩，茯苓桂枝白术甘草汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 67

How to prepare Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang

Decoct with water and take warm in two divided doses.

Cautions

Contraindicated in dizziness due to yin deficiency and hyperactivity of fire.

Questions about Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang

What is Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang used for?

In classical terms Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang treats heart and Spleen yang deficiency, retained phlegm-fluid surging upward. Modern references apply it to chronic congestive heart failure, Ménière's disease, orthostatic hypotension. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang?

Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang (苓桂术甘汤) contains 4 herbs: Fu Ling (poria fungus) 12g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 9g; Bai Zhu (white atractylodes rhizome) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g.

Where does Ling Gui Zhu Gan Tang come from?
