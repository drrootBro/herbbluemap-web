Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang (麻杏甘石汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang — 麻杏甘石汤, the Ephedra, Apricot Kernel, Licorice, and Gypsum Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: exterior pathogen transforming into interior heat, heat congesting the lung qi.

Ingredients of Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang

  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 6g · nodes removed
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 9g · skin and tip removed
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Shi Gao (gypsum) — 18g · crushed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Exterior pathogen transforming into interior heat, heat congesting the lung qi.

Typical pulse: slippery, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Early-stage pneumonia, heat-type wheezing and cough, post-viral bronchitis with yellow sputum.

The original line

After sweating has been promoted, if there is perspiration, wheezing, and absence of extreme high fever: one may administer Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang.

发汗后，汗出而喘，无大热者，可与麻黄杏仁甘草石膏汤。

— Shang Han Lun, line 63

How to prepare Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang

Decoct Ma Huang first, skim off froth, add remaining herbs, and take warm.

Cautions

Contraindicated in cough and wheezing due to deficient cold.

Questions about Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang

What is Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang used for?

In classical terms Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang treats exterior pathogen transforming into interior heat, heat congesting the lung qi. Modern references apply it to early-stage pneumonia, heat-type wheezing and cough, post-viral bronchitis with yellow sputum. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang?

Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang (麻杏甘石汤) contains 4 herbs: Ma Huang (ephedra) 6g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 9g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Shi Gao (gypsum) 18g.

Where does Ma Xing Gan Shi Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 63.
