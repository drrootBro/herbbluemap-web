Da Qing Long Tang (大青龙汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Da Qing Long Tang — 大青龙汤, the Major Bluegreen Dragon Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 7 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: exterior cold with interior heat, repletion both internally and externally.

Ingredients of Da Qing Long Tang

  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 12g · nodes removed
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 6g · bark removed
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 9g · skin and tip removed
  • Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) — 9g · sliced
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 10 pieces · split
  • Shi Gao (gypsum) — 30g · crushed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Da Qing Long Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Exterior cold with interior heat, repletion both internally and externally.

Typical pulse: floating, tight pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Severe flu with high fever, lack of sweat, marked agitation and restlessness, early-stage acute pneumonia.

The original line

In Taiyang wind-strike, with a floating and tight pulse, fever and chills, body aches, absence of perspiration, and vexing agitation: Da Qing Long Tang governs it.

太阳中风，脉浮紧，发热恶寒，身疼痛，不汗出而烦躁者，大青龙汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 38

How to prepare Da Qing Long Tang

Decoct Ma Huang first and skim off froth, add the other herbs, and decoct to obtain a mild sweat.

Cautions

⚠️ Strictly contraindicated in weak, deficient patients. Do not use if sweating is already present, as it can cause devastating collapse of Yang.

Questions about Da Qing Long Tang

What is Da Qing Long Tang used for?

In classical terms Da Qing Long Tang treats exterior cold with interior heat, repletion both internally and externally. Modern references apply it to severe flu with high fever, lack of sweat, marked agitation and restlessness, early-stage acute pneumonia. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Da Qing Long Tang?

Da Qing Long Tang (大青龙汤) contains 7 herbs: Ma Huang (ephedra) 12g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 9g; Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) 9g; Da Zao (jujube) 10 pieces; Shi Gao (gypsum) 30g.

Where does Da Qing Long Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 38.
