Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang (千金苇茎汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang — 千金苇茎汤, the Thousand Pieces of Gold Reed Stem Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Bei Ji Qian Jin Yao Fang. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: heat obstructing lung qi, phlegm-heat clumping in the lung.

Ingredients of Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang

  • Wei Jing (reed stem) — 30g · Lu Gen (reed rhizome) may be substituted
  • Yi Yi Ren (coix seed) — 30g
  • Tao Ren (peach kernel) — 9g · skin and tip removed
  • Dong Gua Ren (winter melon seed) — 24g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Heat obstructing lung qi, phlegm-heat clumping in the lung.

Typical pulse: rapid, slippery pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Post-viral heat-type cough, lung abscess recovery, cough with foul or purulent yellow sputum.

The original line

Treats cough with mild fever, vexation and fullness, and dry scaly cutaneous sensations in the chest, indicating lung abscess: Wei Jing Tang governs it.

治咳有微热，烦满，胸中甲错，是为肺痈，苇茎汤主之。

— Bei Ji Qian Jin Yao Fang

How to prepare Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang

Decoct Wei Jing first, then add other ingredients. Drink warm. Prime choice for copious yellow purulent sputum.

Cautions

Contraindicated in cold cough with clear white phlegm; use with caution in pregnancy.

Questions about Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang

What is Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang used for?

In classical terms Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang treats heat obstructing lung qi, phlegm-heat clumping in the lung. Modern references apply it to post-viral heat-type cough, lung abscess recovery, cough with foul or purulent yellow sputum. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

Qian Jin Wei Jing Tang (千金苇茎汤) contains 4 herbs: Wei Jing (reed stem) 30g; Yi Yi Ren (coix seed) 30g; Tao Ren (peach kernel) 9g; Dong Gua Ren (winter melon seed) 24g.
