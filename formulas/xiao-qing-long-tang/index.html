Xiao Qing Long Tang (小青龙汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Xiao Qing Long Tang — 小青龙汤, the Minor Bluegreen Dragon Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 8 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: exterior cold binding with interior fluid retention, lung qi failing to diffuse.

Ingredients of Xiao Qing Long Tang

  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 9g · nodes removed
  • Bai Shao (white peony root) — 9g
  • Xi Xin (asarum / Chinese wild ginger) — 3g
  • Gan Jiang (dried ginger) — 9g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 9g · bark removed
  • Wu Wei Zi (schisandra fruit) — 6g
  • Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) — 9g · washed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Xiao Qing Long Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Exterior cold binding with interior fluid retention, lung qi failing to diffuse.

Typical pulse: floating, tight or wiry pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Allergic rhinitis, bronchial asthma, cold-type chronic bronchitis.

The original line

When cold damage to the exterior remains unresolved, with water rheum beneath the heart, dry retching, fever, and coughing: Xiao Qing Long Tang governs it.

伤寒表不解，心下有水气，干呕发热而咳，小青龙汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 40

How to prepare Xiao Qing Long Tang

Decoct with water, divide into two warm doses. Reduce dosage immediately once symptoms improve.

Cautions

Contraindicated in cough with yellow, thick phlegm; contraindicated in dry heat cough due to yin deficiency.

Questions about Xiao Qing Long Tang

What is Xiao Qing Long Tang used for?

In classical terms Xiao Qing Long Tang treats exterior cold binding with interior fluid retention, lung qi failing to diffuse. Modern references apply it to allergic rhinitis, bronchial asthma, cold-type chronic bronchitis. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Xiao Qing Long Tang?

Xiao Qing Long Tang (小青龙汤) contains 8 herbs: Ma Huang (ephedra) 9g; Bai Shao (white peony root) 9g; Xi Xin (asarum / Chinese wild ginger) 3g; Gan Jiang (dried ginger) 9g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 9g; Wu Wei Zi (schisandra fruit) 6g; Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) 9g.

Where does Xiao Qing Long Tang come from?
