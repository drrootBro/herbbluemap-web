Jie Geng Tang (桔梗汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Jie Geng Tang — 桔梗汤, the Platycodon Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 2 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: toxic heat obstructing the lung, swollen and painful throat.

Ingredients of Jie Geng Tang

  • Jie Geng (platycodon root) — 6g
  • Gan Cao (raw licorice root) — 6g · raw

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Jie Geng Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Toxic heat obstructing the lung, swollen and painful throat.

Typical pulse: rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute or chronic pharyngitis, tonsillitis, sore throat following a cold.

The original line

In Shaoyin disease with sore throat, Gan Cao Tang may be given; if it does not resolve, administer Jie Geng Tang.

少阴病，咽痛者，可与甘草汤；不差者，与桔梗汤。

— Shang Han Lun, line 311

How to prepare Jie Geng Tang

Decoct with water. Sip slowly and swallow gradually, multiple times throughout the day.

Cautions

Contraindicated in sore throat due to deficient cold.

Questions about Jie Geng Tang

What is Jie Geng Tang used for?

In classical terms Jie Geng Tang treats toxic heat obstructing the lung, swollen and painful throat. Modern references apply it to acute or chronic pharyngitis, tonsillitis, sore throat following a cold. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Jie Geng Tang?

Jie Geng Tang (桔梗汤) contains 2 herbs: Jie Geng (platycodon root) 6g; Gan Cao (raw licorice root) 6g.

Where does Jie Geng Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 311.
