Mai Men Dong Tang (麦门冬汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Mai Men Dong Tang — 麦门冬汤, the Ophiopogon Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 6 herbs from the Jin Gui Yao Lue. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: yin deficiency of Lung and Stomach, deficiency fire flaring upward.

Ingredients of Mai Men Dong Tang

  • Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) — 30g
  • Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) — 6g
  • Ren Shen (ginseng) — 6g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Geng Mi (nonglutinous round-grain rice) — 6g
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 12 pieces · split

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Mai Men Dong Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Yin deficiency of Lung and Stomach, deficiency fire flaring upward.

Typical pulse: deficient, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Dry irritating cough after cold, chronic pharyngitis, convalescence of bronchitis.

The original line

For counterflow qi ascent with distress in the throat, to arrest counterflow and bring down the rebellious qi: Mai Men Dong Tang governs it.

大逆上气，咽喉不利，止逆下气者，麦门冬汤主之。

— Jin Gui Yao Lue

How to prepare Mai Men Dong Tang

Decoct with water and divide into three warm doses across the day.

Cautions

Caution in cough with copious phlegm or dampness; contraindicated in cold-type cough.

Questions about Mai Men Dong Tang

What is Mai Men Dong Tang used for?

In classical terms Mai Men Dong Tang treats yin deficiency of Lung and Stomach, deficiency fire flaring upward. Modern references apply it to dry irritating cough after cold, chronic pharyngitis, convalescence of bronchitis. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Mai Men Dong Tang?

Mai Men Dong Tang (麦门冬汤) contains 6 herbs: Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) 30g; Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) 6g; Ren Shen (ginseng) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Geng Mi (nonglutinous round-grain rice) 6g; Da Zao (jujube) 12 pieces.

Where does Mai Men Dong Tang come from?

Jin Gui Yao Lue.
