Ma Zi Ren Wan (麻子仁丸): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ma Zi Ren Wan — 麻子仁丸, the Hemp Seed Pill — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 6 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: spleen constraint pattern (Pi Yue), dryness of body fluids in the large intestine.

Ingredients of Ma Zi Ren Wan

  • Huo Ma Ren (hemp seed) — 15g
  • Bai Shao (white peony root) — 9g
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) — 9g · dry-fried
  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 9g · bark removed
  • Hou Po (magnolia bark) — 9g · dry-fried
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 9g · skin and tip removed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ma Zi Ren Wan is used for

Classical pattern: Spleen constraint pattern (Pi Yue), dryness of body fluids in the large intestine.

Typical pulse: floating, choppy pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Geriatric constipation, postoperative constipation, habitual chronic constipation.

The original line

Fu-yang pulse is floating and choppy... floating indicates strong stomach qi, choppy indicates frequent urination. Hard stool with constrained spleen: Ma Zi Ren Wan governs it.

趺阳脉浮而涩，浮则胃气强，涩则小便数，大便则硬，其脾为约，麻子仁丸主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 247

How to prepare Ma Zi Ren Wan

Traditionally prepared as honey pills; can be decocted in modern practice. Reduce dose as soon as regular bowel movements return.

Cautions

Use with caution in pregnancy; reduce dosage in weak patients. Primary choice for chronic constipation in the elderly.

Questions about Ma Zi Ren Wan

What is Ma Zi Ren Wan used for?

In classical terms Ma Zi Ren Wan treats spleen constraint pattern (Pi Yue), dryness of body fluids in the large intestine. Modern references apply it to geriatric constipation, postoperative constipation, habitual chronic constipation. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ma Zi Ren Wan?

Ma Zi Ren Wan (麻子仁丸) contains 6 herbs: Huo Ma Ren (hemp seed) 15g; Bai Shao (white peony root) 9g; Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) 9g; Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 9g; Hou Po (magnolia bark) 9g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 9g.
