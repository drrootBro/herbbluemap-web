Ze Xie Tang (泽泻汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ze Xie Tang — 泽泻汤, the Alisma Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 2 herbs from the Jin Gui Yao Lue. It belongs to the Taiyin (water-rheum) stage and is aimed at one pattern: propping rheum below the heart; turbid fluid blocks clear yang from rising to the head.

Two herbs, one job: Ze Xie drains the water that is clouding the head, Bai Zhu strengthens the spleen so the water stops collecting. It is one of the shortest formulas in the classics and a favorite teaching example of how ratio, not herb count, makes a formula.

Ingredients of Ze Xie Tang

  • Ze Xie (alisma rhizome) — 15g · classical ratio 5 liang
  • Bai Zhu (white atractylodes rhizome) — 6g · classical ratio 2 liang

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ze Xie Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Propping rheum below the heart; turbid fluid blocks clear yang from rising to the head.

Typical pulse: wiry or submerged pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Vertigo and dizziness from fluid retention, Ménière's disease, positional vertigo with heavy head.

The original line

When there is propping rheum below the heart and the person suffers from veiling dizziness: Ze Xie Tang governs it.

心下有支饮，其人苦冒眩，泽泻汤主之。

— Jin Gui Yao Lue, Phlegm-Rheum chapter

How to prepare Ze Xie Tang

Decoct the two herbs in about 2 cups of water down to 1 cup; take warm, split into two doses. The 5:2 ratio of Ze Xie to Bai Zhu is the point of the formula.

Cautions

Not for dizziness from blood or yin deficiency without fluid retention. Dizziness can signal stroke, heart or inner-ear disease — get it assessed first.

Questions about Ze Xie Tang

What is Ze Xie Tang used for?

In classical terms Ze Xie Tang treats propping rheum below the heart; turbid fluid blocks clear yang from rising to the head. Modern references apply it to vertigo and dizziness from fluid retention, Ménière's disease, positional vertigo with heavy head. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ze Xie Tang?

Ze Xie Tang (泽泻汤) contains 2 herbs: Ze Xie (alisma rhizome) 15g; Bai Zhu (white atractylodes rhizome) 6g.

Where does Ze Xie Tang come from?
