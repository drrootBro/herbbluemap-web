Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang (葛根黄芩黄连汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang — 葛根黄芩黄连汤, the Kudzu, Scutellaria, and Coptis Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: exterior pathogen penetrating interior to generate heat, heat forcing down the large intestine.

Ingredients of Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang

  • Ge Gen (kudzu root) — 15g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Huang Qin (scutellaria / baical skullcap root) — 9g
  • Huang Lian (coptis rhizome) — 9g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Exterior pathogen penetrating interior to generate heat, heat forcing down the large intestine.

Typical pulse: rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute gastroenteritis, bacterial dysentery, heat-pattern diarrhea.

The original line

Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang governs it.

葛根黄芩黄连汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 34

How to prepare Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang

Boil Ge Gen first, then add the other herbs and decoct together. Take warm.

Cautions

Contraindicated in cold-pattern watery diarrhea. Foul-smelling stool with burning sensation around the anus is key.

Questions about Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang

What is Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang used for?

In classical terms Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang treats exterior pathogen penetrating interior to generate heat, heat forcing down the large intestine. Modern references apply it to acute gastroenteritis, bacterial dysentery, heat-pattern diarrhea. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang?

Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang (葛根黄芩黄连汤) contains 4 herbs: Ge Gen (kudzu root) 15g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Huang Qin (scutellaria / baical skullcap root) 9g; Huang Lian (coptis rhizome) 9g.

Where does Ge Gen Huang Qin Huang Lian Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 34.
