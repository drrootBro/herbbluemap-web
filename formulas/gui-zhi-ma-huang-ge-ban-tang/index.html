Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang (桂枝麻黄各半汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang — 桂枝麻黄各半汤, the Cinnamon Twig and Ephedra Half-and-Half Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 7 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: mild Taiyang pattern, minor constraint of nutritive and defensive qi.

Ingredients of Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang

  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 4.5g · bark removed
  • Bai Shao (white peony root) — 3g
  • Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) — 3g · sliced
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 3g
  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 3g · nodes removed
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 4 pieces · split
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 6g · skin and tip removed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Mild Taiyang pattern, minor constraint of nutritive and defensive qi.

Typical pulse: floating, fine, and moderate pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Mild exterior cold, allergic skin itching, urticaria with low-grade fever.

The original line

If the body must itch, Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang is indicated.

身必痒，宜桂枝麻黄各半汤。

— Shang Han Lun, line 23

How to prepare Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang

Decoct Ma Huang first, remove foam, add other herbs and decoct. Take warm to achieve a very slight sweat.

Cautions

Caution in deficient individuals; contraindicated if copious sweating is present.

Questions about Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang

What is Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang used for?

In classical terms Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang treats mild Taiyang pattern, minor constraint of nutritive and defensive qi. Modern references apply it to mild exterior cold, allergic skin itching, urticaria with low-grade fever. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang?

Gui Zhi Ma Huang Ge Ban Tang (桂枝麻黄各半汤) contains 7 herbs: Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 4.5g; Bai Shao (white peony root) 3g; Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) 3g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 3g; Ma Huang (ephedra) 3g; Da Zao (jujube) 4 pieces; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 6g.
