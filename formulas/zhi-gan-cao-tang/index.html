Zhi Gan Cao Tang (炙甘草汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Zhi Gan Cao Tang — 炙甘草汤, the Honey-Fried Licorice Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 9 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: dual deficiency of heart yin and yang, depletion of qi and blood.

Ingredients of Zhi Gan Cao Tang

  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 12g
  • Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) — 9g
  • Ren Shen (ginseng) — 6g
  • Sheng Di Huang (raw rehmannia root) — 30g
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 9g · bark removed
  • E Jiao (donkey-hide gelatin) — 6g · melted into strained liquid
  • Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) — 10g · core removed
  • Huo Ma Ren (hemp seed) — 10g
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 30 pieces · split

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Zhi Gan Cao Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Dual deficiency of heart yin and yang, depletion of qi and blood.

Typical pulse: knotted, intermittent pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Cardiac arrhythmias, premature beats, atrial fibrillation, bradycardia.

The original line

In cold damage with a knotted, intermittent pulse and heart palpitations: Zhi Gan Cao Tang governs it.

伤寒脉结代，心动悸，炙甘草汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 177

How to prepare Zhi Gan Cao Tang

Decoct herbs in half water and half rice wine. Melt E Jiao separately into the strained warm liquid before taking.

Cautions

Contraindicated in excess heat patterns.

Questions about Zhi Gan Cao Tang

What is Zhi Gan Cao Tang used for?

In classical terms Zhi Gan Cao Tang treats dual deficiency of heart yin and yang, depletion of qi and blood. Modern references apply it to cardiac arrhythmias, premature beats, atrial fibrillation, bradycardia. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Zhi Gan Cao Tang?

Zhi Gan Cao Tang (炙甘草汤) contains 9 herbs: Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 12g; Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) 9g; Ren Shen (ginseng) 6g; Sheng Di Huang (raw rehmannia root) 30g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 9g; E Jiao (donkey-hide gelatin) 6g; Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) 10g; Huo Ma Ren (hemp seed) 10g; Da Zao (jujube) 30 pieces.

Where does Zhi Gan Cao Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 177.
