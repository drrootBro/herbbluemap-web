Chinese Herbal Formulas A–Z

Chinese medicine rarely prescribes a single herb. It prescribes a formula: a small team of herbs aimed at one pattern. These are the classical formulas of the Shang Han Lun and its companion texts — each with its pinyin and Chinese name, ingredients, typical uses, the original line, and cautions. Every herb links to its page in the herb library.
