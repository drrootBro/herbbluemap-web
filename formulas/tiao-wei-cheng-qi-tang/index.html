Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang (调胃承气汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang — 调胃承气汤, the Stomach-Regulating Decoction to Order the Qi — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 3 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: mild Yangming bowel repletion, initial accumulation of dry heat.

Ingredients of Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang

  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 12g · added in the last 5 minutes
  • Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) — 9g · dissolved into strained decoction
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Mild Yangming bowel repletion, initial accumulation of dry heat.

Typical pulse: slippery, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Mild constipation, dry stools from stomach heat, halitosis.

The original line

In Yangming disease, if there is neither vomiting nor diarrhea, but mental vexation is present: Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang may be given.

阳明病，不吐不下，心烦者，可与调胃承气汤。

— Shang Han Lun, line 207

How to prepare Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang

Decoct herbs, adding Da Huang near the end. Strain, dissolve Mang Xiao in the hot liquid, and take warm. Stop once bowel movement occurs.

Cautions

Contraindicated in debility and pregnancy; mildest purge, best when there is dry stool without marked abdominal distension.

Questions about Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang

What is Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang used for?

In classical terms Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang treats mild Yangming bowel repletion, initial accumulation of dry heat. Modern references apply it to mild constipation, dry stools from stomach heat, halitosis. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang?

Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang (调胃承气汤) contains 3 herbs: Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 12g; Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) 9g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g.
