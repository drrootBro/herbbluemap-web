Xiao Cheng Qi Tang (小承气汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Xiao Cheng Qi Tang — 小承气汤, the Minor Decoction to Order the Qi — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 3 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: moderate Yangming bowel repletion, obstructed bowel qi movement.

Ingredients of Xiao Cheng Qi Tang

  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 12g · added in the last 5 minutes
  • Hou Po (magnolia bark) — 6g · bark removed
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) — 9g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Xiao Cheng Qi Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Moderate Yangming bowel repletion, obstructed bowel qi movement.

Typical pulse: slippery, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Moderate constipation, abdominal distension and bloating, food stagnation.

The original line

In Yangming disease, if the person sweats profusely, body fluids leak out causing dryness in the stomach, and stool will surely harden: Xiao Cheng Qi Tang governs it.

阳明病，其人多汗，以津液外出，胃中燥，大便必硬，小承气汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 213

How to prepare Xiao Cheng Qi Tang

Decoct Hou Po and Zhi Shi first, add Da Huang near the end. Take warm and stop once bowels move.

Cautions

Contraindicated in debility and pregnancy; if abdominal distension is absent, use Tiao Wei Cheng Qi Tang instead.

Questions about Xiao Cheng Qi Tang

What is Xiao Cheng Qi Tang used for?

In classical terms Xiao Cheng Qi Tang treats moderate Yangming bowel repletion, obstructed bowel qi movement. Modern references apply it to moderate constipation, abdominal distension and bloating, food stagnation. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Xiao Cheng Qi Tang?

Xiao Cheng Qi Tang (小承气汤) contains 3 herbs: Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 12g; Hou Po (magnolia bark) 6g; Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) 9g.

Where does Xiao Cheng Qi Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 213.
