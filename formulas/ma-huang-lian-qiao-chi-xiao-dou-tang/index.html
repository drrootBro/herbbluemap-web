Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang (麻黄连翘赤小豆汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang — 麻黄连翘赤小豆汤, the Ephedra, Forsythia, and Adzuki Bean Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 8 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: damp-heat constrained in the exterior, unable to vent outward.

Ingredients of Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang

  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 6g · nodes removed
  • Lian Qiao (forsythia fruit) — 6g
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 6g · skin and tip removed
  • Chi Xiao Dou (adzuki bean) — 30g
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 12 pieces · split
  • Sheng Zi Bai Pi (fresh catalpa bark) — 9g · Sang Bai Pi (mulberry root bark) may be substituted
  • Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) — 6g · sliced
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Damp-heat constrained in the exterior, unable to vent outward.

Typical pulse: floating, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute eczema, urticaria, pruritic dermatosis accompanied by mild jaundice or heat.

The original line

In cold damage, when stagnant heat remains in the interior and jaundice must appear: Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang governs it.

伤寒，瘀热在里，身必黄，麻黄连翘赤小豆汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 262

How to prepare Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang

Decoct Ma Huang first and skim froth, add the other herbs, decoct, and take warm.

Cautions

Contraindicated in dry pruritic skin disorders due to yin and blood deficiency.

Questions about Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang

What is Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang used for?

In classical terms Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang treats damp-heat constrained in the exterior, unable to vent outward. Modern references apply it to acute eczema, urticaria, pruritic dermatosis accompanied by mild jaundice or heat. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang?

Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang (麻黄连翘赤小豆汤) contains 8 herbs: Ma Huang (ephedra) 6g; Lian Qiao (forsythia fruit) 6g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 6g; Chi Xiao Dou (adzuki bean) 30g; Da Zao (jujube) 12 pieces; Sheng Zi Bai Pi (fresh catalpa bark) 9g; Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g.

Where does Ma Huang Lian Qiao Chi Xiao Dou Tang come from?
