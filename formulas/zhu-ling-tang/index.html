Zhu Ling Tang (猪苓汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Zhu Ling Tang — 猪苓汤, the Polyporus Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 5 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: yin deficiency complicated by clumping of water and heat.

Ingredients of Zhu Ling Tang

  • Zhu Ling (polyporus sclerotium) — 9g · bark removed
  • Fu Ling (poria fungus) — 9g
  • Ze Xie (alisma rhizome) — 9g
  • E Jiao (donkey-hide gelatin) — 9g · melted into strained liquid
  • Hua Shi (talcum powder) — 9g · crushed, bagged

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Zhu Ling Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Yin deficiency complicated by clumping of water and heat.

Typical pulse: fine, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Urinary tract infection, nephrolithiasis, acute nephritis, hematuria with dysuria.

The original line

If there is a floating pulse, fever, thirst with desire to drink, and difficulty urinating: Zhu Ling Tang governs it.

若脉浮发热，渴欲饮水，小便不利者，猪苓汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 223

How to prepare Zhu Ling Tang

Decoct herbs in water, strain, and dissolve E Jiao into the warm decoction.

Cautions

Use with caution in Spleen and Stomach deficiency cold.

Questions about Zhu Ling Tang

What is Zhu Ling Tang used for?

In classical terms Zhu Ling Tang treats yin deficiency complicated by clumping of water and heat. Modern references apply it to urinary tract infection, nephrolithiasis, acute nephritis, hematuria with dysuria. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Zhu Ling Tang?

Zhu Ling Tang (猪苓汤) contains 5 herbs: Zhu Ling (polyporus sclerotium) 9g; Fu Ling (poria fungus) 9g; Ze Xie (alisma rhizome) 9g; E Jiao (donkey-hide gelatin) 9g; Hua Shi (talcum powder) 9g.

Where does Zhu Ling Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 223.
