Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang (竹叶石膏汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang — 竹叶石膏汤, the Lophatherum and Gypsum Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 7 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: lingering residual heat injuring both qi and yin.

Ingredients of Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang

  • Zhu Ye (lophatherum bamboo leaf) — 6g
  • Shi Gao (gypsum) — 30g
  • Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) — 9g · washed
  • Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) — 20g · core removed
  • Ren Shen (ginseng) — 6g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Geng Mi (nonglutinous round-grain rice) — 9g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Lingering residual heat injuring both qi and yin.

Typical pulse: deficient, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Prolonged low-grade fever after a viral infection, convalescent debility with thirst.

The original line

After resolution of cold damage, if the patient is emaciated, short of breath, and has counterflow retching: Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang governs it.

伤寒解后，虚羸少气，气逆欲吐，竹叶石膏汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 397

How to prepare Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang

Decoct in water until the rice is thoroughly cooked, strain off the dregs, and take warm.

Cautions

Use with caution in individuals with Spleen and Stomach deficiency-cold.

Questions about Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang

What is Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang used for?

In classical terms Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang treats lingering residual heat injuring both qi and yin. Modern references apply it to prolonged low-grade fever after a viral infection, convalescent debility with thirst. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang?

Zhu Ye Shi Gao Tang (竹叶石膏汤) contains 7 herbs: Zhu Ye (lophatherum bamboo leaf) 6g; Shi Gao (gypsum) 30g; Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) 9g; Mai Dong (ophiopogon tuber) 20g; Ren Shen (ginseng) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Geng Mi (nonglutinous round-grain rice) 9g.
