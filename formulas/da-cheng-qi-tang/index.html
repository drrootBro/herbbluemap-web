Da Cheng Qi Tang (大承气汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Da Cheng Qi Tang — 大承气汤, the Major Decoction to Order the Qi — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: severe Yangming bowel repletion, severe binding of dry feces with heat.

Ingredients of Da Cheng Qi Tang

  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 12g · added in the last 5 minutes
  • Hou Po (magnolia bark) — 15g · bark removed
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) — 12g
  • Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) — 9g · dissolved into strained decoction

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Da Cheng Qi Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Severe Yangming bowel repletion, severe binding of dry feces with heat.

Typical pulse: submerged, full, forceful pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute intestinal obstruction, severe obstipation, toxic paralytic ileus.

The original line

In Yangming disease, with delirious speech and tidal fever, if the patient conversely cannot eat, there must be five or six dry fecal balls in the stomach: purge with Da Cheng Qi Tang.

阳明病，谵语有潮热，反不能食者，胃中必有燥屎五六枚，宜大承气汤下之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 209

How to prepare Da Cheng Qi Tang

Decoct Hou Po and Zhi Shi first, then add Da Huang. Strain, dissolve Mang Xiao into the liquid, and take warm. Cease taking once purging occurs.

Cautions

⚠️ Strictly contraindicated in pregnancy and physical exhaustion. Acute surgical abdomen requires simultaneous emergency hospital evaluation.

Questions about Da Cheng Qi Tang

What is Da Cheng Qi Tang used for?

In classical terms Da Cheng Qi Tang treats severe Yangming bowel repletion, severe binding of dry feces with heat. Modern references apply it to acute intestinal obstruction, severe obstipation, toxic paralytic ileus. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Da Cheng Qi Tang?

Da Cheng Qi Tang (大承气汤) contains 4 herbs: Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 12g; Hou Po (magnolia bark) 15g; Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) 12g; Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) 9g.

Where does Da Cheng Qi Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 209.
