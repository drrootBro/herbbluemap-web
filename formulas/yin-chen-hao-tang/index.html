Yin Chen Hao Tang (茵陈蒿汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Yin Chen Hao Tang — 茵陈蒿汤, the Artemisia Yinchenhao Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 3 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: damp-heat steaming and causing yellowing, Yang Jaundice pattern.

Ingredients of Yin Chen Hao Tang

  • Yin Chen (capillaris / yinchen wormwood) — 18g
  • Zhi Zi (gardenia fruit) — 12g · crushed
  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 6g · bark removed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Yin Chen Hao Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Damp-heat steaming and causing yellowing, Yang Jaundice pattern.

Typical pulse: submerged, rapid pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute/chronic hepatitis, cholecystitis, icteric jaundice.

The original line

The body will inevitably turn yellow: Yin Chen Hao Tang governs it.

身必发黄，茵陈蒿汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 236

How to prepare Yin Chen Hao Tang

Boil Yin Chen first, then add Zhi Zi and Da Huang and decoct together. Take warm.

Cautions

Contraindicated in Yin Jaundice (cold-dampness); use with caution in Spleen-deficient loose stools.

Questions about Yin Chen Hao Tang

What is Yin Chen Hao Tang used for?

In classical terms Yin Chen Hao Tang treats damp-heat steaming and causing yellowing, Yang Jaundice pattern. Modern references apply it to acute/chronic hepatitis, cholecystitis, icteric jaundice. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Yin Chen Hao Tang?

Yin Chen Hao Tang (茵陈蒿汤) contains 3 herbs: Yin Chen (capillaris / yinchen wormwood) 18g; Zhi Zi (gardenia fruit) 12g; Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 6g.

Where does Yin Chen Hao Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 236.
