Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang (苓甘五味姜辛夏仁汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang — 苓甘五味姜辛夏仁汤, the Poria, Licorice, Dried Ginger, Schisandra, Asarum, Pinellia, and Apricot Kernel Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 7 herbs from the Jin Gui Yao Lue (Phlegm-Rheum Cough). It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: cold rheum congesting the Lung, loss of diffusion and downward regulation.

Ingredients of Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang

  • Fu Ling (poria fungus) — 12g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 9g
  • Gan Jiang (dried ginger) — 9g
  • Wu Wei Zi (schisandra fruit) — 6g
  • Xi Xin (asarum / Chinese wild ginger) — 3g
  • Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) — 9g · washed
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 9g · skin and tip removed

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Cold rheum congesting the Lung, loss of diffusion and downward regulation.

Typical pulse: wiry, slippery pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Post-viral cold-type chronic cough, chronic bronchitis with cold rheum.

The original line

After Xiao Qing Long Tang has been administered and the surging qi abates, yet cough and chest fullness worsen... Ling Gan Wu Wei Jiang Xin Xia Ren Tang governs it.

青龙汤下已，冲气即低，而反更咳、胸满者……苓甘五味姜辛夏仁汤主之。

— Jin Gui Yao Lue (Phlegm-Rheum Cough)

How to prepare Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang

Decoct with water and take warm in two doses. Highly suitable for profuse clear white sputum aggravated by cold exposure.

Cautions

Contraindicated in heat cough with yellow phlegm; contraindicated in dry cough from yin deficiency.

Questions about Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang

What is Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang used for?

In classical terms Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang treats cold rheum congesting the Lung, loss of diffusion and downward regulation. Modern references apply it to post-viral cold-type chronic cough, chronic bronchitis with cold rheum. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang?

Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang (苓甘五味姜辛夏仁汤) contains 7 herbs: Fu Ling (poria fungus) 12g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 9g; Gan Jiang (dried ginger) 9g; Wu Wei Zi (schisandra fruit) 6g; Xi Xin (asarum / Chinese wild ginger) 3g; Ban Xia (pinellia rhizome) 9g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 9g.

Where does Ling Gan Jiang Wei Xin Xia Ren Tang come from?

Jin Gui Yao Lue (Phlegm-Rheum Cough).
