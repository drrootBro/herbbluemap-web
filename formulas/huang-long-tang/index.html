Huang Long Tang (黄龙汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Huang Long Tang — 黄龙汤, the Yellow Dragon Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 10 herbs from the Shang Han Liu Shu (Tao Hua. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: yangming bowel repletion (heat binding) with qi and blood deficiency — purge and support at the same time.

The name says it: a 'dragon' of purging herbs (the Da Cheng Qi Tang core) carried by ginseng and angelica, so that a patient too weak for a plain purge can still have the blockage cleared. It is the classic answer to the dilemma 'he needs a purge, but he cannot survive one.'

Ingredients of Huang Long Tang

  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 9g · added late
  • Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) — 9g · dissolved into strained decoction
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) — 9g
  • Hou Po (magnolia bark) — 9g
  • Ren Shen (ginseng) — 6g
  • Dang Gui (Chinese angelica root) — 9g
  • Gan Cao (raw licorice root) — 3g
  • Jie Geng (platycodon root) — 3g
  • Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) — 3 slices
  • Da Zao (jujube) — 2 pieces

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Huang Long Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Yangming bowel repletion (heat binding) with qi and blood deficiency — purge and support at the same time.

Typical pulse: deficient, or submerged and forceless pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Constipation or foul watery 'overflow' diarrhea in weak, elderly or exhausted patients; bowel obstruction with collapse of strength.

The original line

Built on Da Cheng Qi Tang (Shang Han Lun line 209) with Ren Shen and Dang Gui added, for heat binding in the bowels when the patient is too weak to bear a purge alone.

热结旁流，或大便秘结，腹痛拒按，身热口渴，神疲少气，宜黄龙汤。

— Shang Han Liu Shu (Tao Hua, Ming dynasty), building on Da Cheng Qi Tang

How to prepare Huang Long Tang

Decoct Zhi Shi, Hou Po, Ren Shen, Dang Gui, Gan Cao, Jie Geng, Sheng Jiang and Da Zao; add Da Huang near the end; strain and dissolve Mang Xiao. Stop once the bowels move.

Cautions

A strong purgative: not in pregnancy, not without real bowel repletion. Severe abdominal pain, vomiting or no bowel movement with distension can be a surgical emergency — go to a hospital first.

Questions about Huang Long Tang

What is Huang Long Tang used for?

In classical terms Huang Long Tang treats yangming bowel repletion (heat binding) with qi and blood deficiency — purge and support at the same time. Modern references apply it to constipation or foul watery 'overflow' diarrhea in weak, elderly or exhausted patients; bowel obstruction with collapse of strength. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Huang Long Tang?

Huang Long Tang (黄龙汤) contains 10 herbs: Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 9g; Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) 9g; Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange) 9g; Hou Po (magnolia bark) 9g; Ren Shen (ginseng) 6g; Dang Gui (Chinese angelica root) 9g; Gan Cao (raw licorice root) 3g; Jie Geng (platycodon root) 3g; Sheng Jiang (fresh ginger) 3 slices; Da Zao (jujube) 2 pieces.

Where does Huang Long Tang come from?

Shang Han Liu Shu (Tao Hua, Ming dynasty), building on Da Cheng Qi Tang.
