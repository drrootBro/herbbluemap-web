Tao He Cheng Qi Tang (桃核承气汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Tao He Cheng Qi Tang — 桃核承气汤, the Peach Pit Decoction to Order the Qi — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 5 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: taiyang blood amassment, mutual clumping of stasis and interior heat.

Ingredients of Tao He Cheng Qi Tang

  • Tao Ren (peach kernel) — 12g · skin and tip removed
  • Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) — 12g · added in the last 5 minutes
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 6g · bark removed
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 6g
  • Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) — 6g · dissolved into strained decoction

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Tao He Cheng Qi Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Taiyang blood amassment, mutual clumping of stasis and interior heat.

Typical pulse: submerged, choppy pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Acute pelvic inflammatory disease, dysmenorrhea (blood stasis heat type), emotional mania.

The original line

When heat binds in the urinary bladder, and the patient acts as if manic, with acute urgency and tension in the lower abdomen: Tao He Cheng Qi Tang governs it.

热结膀胱，其人如狂，少腹急结者，桃核承气汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 106

How to prepare Tao He Cheng Qi Tang

Decoct with water, adding Da Huang near the end. Strain, dissolve Mang Xiao into the hot liquid, and take warm. Discontinue as soon as bowels open.

Cautions

⚠️ Strictly contraindicated during pregnancy; contraindicated in weak, deficient patients.

Questions about Tao He Cheng Qi Tang

What is Tao He Cheng Qi Tang used for?

In classical terms Tao He Cheng Qi Tang treats taiyang blood amassment, mutual clumping of stasis and interior heat. Modern references apply it to acute pelvic inflammatory disease, dysmenorrhea (blood stasis heat type), emotional mania. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Tao He Cheng Qi Tang?

Tao He Cheng Qi Tang (桃核承气汤) contains 5 herbs: Tao Ren (peach kernel) 12g; Da Huang (rhubarb rhizome) 12g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 6g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 6g; Mang Xiao (mirabilite / sodium sulfate) 6g.

Where does Tao He Cheng Qi Tang come from?
