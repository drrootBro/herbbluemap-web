Ma Huang Tang (麻黄汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Ma Huang Tang — 麻黄汤, the Ephedra Decoction — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 4 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Taiyang (Greater Yang) stage and is aimed at one pattern: taiyang cold damage, cold pathogen binding the exterior.

Ingredients of Ma Huang Tang

  • Ma Huang (ephedra) — 9g · nodes removed
  • Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) — 6g · bark removed
  • Xing Ren (apricot kernel) — 12g · skin and tip removed
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 3g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Ma Huang Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Taiyang cold damage, cold pathogen binding the exterior.

Typical pulse: floating, tight pulse.

Modern clinical uses: Influenza (anhidrotic type), acute upper respiratory tract infection.

The original line

In Taiyang disease, with headache, fever, bodily aching, lower back pain, aching joints, aversion to wind, absence of sweating, and wheezing: Ma Huang Tang governs it.

太阳病，头痛发热，身疼腰痛，骨节疼痛，恶风无汗而喘者，麻黄汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 35

How to prepare Ma Huang Tang

Decoct Ma Huang first, skim off the froth, then add the other herbs. Take warm, cover with blankets, and stop once a light sweat breaks out.

Cautions

⚠️ Strictly contraindicated if sweating is present. Use with extreme caution in deficient patients, the elderly, and pregnant women.

Questions about Ma Huang Tang

What is Ma Huang Tang used for?

In classical terms Ma Huang Tang treats taiyang cold damage, cold pathogen binding the exterior. Modern references apply it to influenza (anhidrotic type), acute upper respiratory tract infection. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.

What are the ingredients of Ma Huang Tang?

Ma Huang Tang (麻黄汤) contains 4 herbs: Ma Huang (ephedra) 9g; Gui Zhi (cinnamon twig) 6g; Xing Ren (apricot kernel) 12g; Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) 3g.

Where does Ma Huang Tang come from?

Shang Han Lun, line 35.
