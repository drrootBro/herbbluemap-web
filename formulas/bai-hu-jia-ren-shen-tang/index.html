Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang (白虎加人参汤): Ingredients, Uses & Original Text

Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang — 白虎加人参汤, the White Tiger Decoction with Ginseng — is a classical Chinese herbal formula of 5 herbs from the Shang Han Lun. It belongs to the Yangming (Yang Brightness) stage and is aimed at one pattern: yangming blazing heat with dual impairment of qi and body fluids.

Ingredients of Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang

  • Shi Gao (gypsum) — 30g · crushed
  • Zhi Mu (anemarrhena rhizome) — 9g
  • Zhi Gan Cao (honey-fried licorice) — 3g
  • Geng Mi (nonglutinous round-grain rice) — 9g
  • Ren Shen (ginseng) — 9g

Doses are typical modern textbook amounts for one day's decoction, shown for study. They are not a prescription.

What Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang is used for

Classical pattern: Yangming blazing heat with dual impairment of qi and body fluids.

Typical pulse: flooding, large, yet deficient pulse.

Modern clinical uses: High fever with qi exhaustion, diabetic polydipsia, severe prostration after sunstroke.

The original line

After taking Gui Zhi Tang with profuse perspiration, if severe vexing thirst remains unquenched and the pulse is flooding and large: Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang governs it.

服桂枝汤，大汗出后，大烦渴不解，脉洪大者，白虎加人参汤主之。

— Shang Han Lun, line 26

How to prepare Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang

Decoct with water until rice is cooked, strain, and take warm.

Cautions

Contraindicated in Spleen-Stomach deficient cold; contraindicated without heat signs.

Questions about Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang

What is Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang used for?

In classical terms Bai Hu Jia Ren Shen Tang treats yangming blazing heat with dual impairment of qi and body fluids. Modern references apply it to high fever with qi exhaustion, diabetic polydipsia, severe prostration after sunstroke. It should be chosen by pattern, by a qualified practitioner — not by a disease name alone.
